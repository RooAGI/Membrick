# Accessibility

We want Membrick to be usable by as many people as possible.

## Commitment

The command line tools, documentation, and local dashboard should work with
keyboard navigation and common assistive technology. We try to follow the
WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines for the dashboard and the documentation site.

## Supported environments

- Command line: any terminal on macOS, Linux, and Windows. Output is plain
  text and does not rely on color alone.
- Dashboard and docs: current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge,
  with a screen reader such as VoiceOver, NVDA, or JAWS.

## Known limitations

- Some dashboard views, such as charts and graphs, may not have full text
  alternatives yet.
- Keyboard focus order and contrast may not meet WCAG 2.1 AA everywhere in the
  dashboard.

## Report a barrier

If you find something hard to use, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/RooAGI/Membrick/issues) or email
lw@rooagi.com. Tell us the page or command, your browser or terminal, any
assistive technology you use, and what went wrong. We will review reports and
fix what we can.
